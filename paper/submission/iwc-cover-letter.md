# Cover letter — Interacting with Computers

Dear Editors,

Please consider our Short Research Paper, “Separating Interpretation from Authority:
Bounded Local Adaptation for Desktop Automation by Demonstration,” for publication in
*Interacting with Computers*.

The paper presents Neloa, an open-source macOS system that lets a person demonstrate a
workflow and later change bounded details such as dates, amounts, files, and form
values. The system uses a local multimodal model for interpretation while deterministic
software constrains the executable result to a reviewed action graph and preserves
approval gates. We report a frozen, fully reproducible 15-case formative benchmark of
two quantization tiers, including repeated-run outcomes and local resource measurements.

The work is relevant to the journal’s readership because it examines human-centred AI,
mixed-initiative interaction, end-user automation, privacy, and the legibility of agent
authority. We state the evaluation boundary explicitly: the study uses synthetic cases
and exact executable assertions, with no claims about arbitrary desktop reliability or
end-user usability.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and contains no
human-participant or personal-recording data. The source, frozen protocol, synthetic
fixtures, raw reports, and analysis code are available in the project repository. Any
generative-AI assistance is disclosed in the manuscript; the human author has reviewed
the work and accepts responsibility for its accuracy and originality.
