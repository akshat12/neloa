# HCII 2027 proposal upload map

1. Select the **AI-HCI** thematic area in the HCII Conference Management System.
2. Upload `neloa-hcii-2027-proposal.pdf` as the paper proposal.
3. Enter the full author and contact details in the CMS; HCII uses single-blind review.
4. The proposal contains 767 words excluding references, under the 800-word limit.
5. Submit by **9 October 2026, Anywhere on Earth**.
6. Before committing to submission, confirm with `program@2027.hci.international` that
   an accepted regular paper may be presented remotely. The conference advertises
   online participation but its regular-paper page does not explicitly state the remote
   presentation rule.

If the proposal is accepted, a 10–20 page camera-ready paper (typically 12 pages) in an
editable Springer-compatible format and PDF will be due 29 January 2027. At least one
author registration is required for presentation and proceedings inclusion.
